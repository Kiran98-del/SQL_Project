📱 Instagram User Data Analysis – Marketing Strategy Support

**Problem Statement**

I was assigned to collaborate with the Marketing team to analyze Instagram user data. The objective was to uncover insights that would support the development of targeted marketing strategies focused on improving user engagement, retention, and acquisition.

**Project Objectives**

Analyze user behavior and content interaction trends

Segment users based on demographics, engagement levels, and interests

Identify key drivers behind user engagement and retention

Detect churn patterns and possible areas of user drop-off

Provide actionable, data-driven recommendations to optimize marketing strategies

**Tools Used**

MySQL

Microsoft Excel

Power BI 

**Key Insights**

Reels and Stories were the most engaging content types across user segments

Weekends and late evenings were peak times for user activity

A significant churn risk was observed among users with low posting frequency

Personalized content recommendations had a strong positive impact on retention

**Recommendations**

Launch segmented campaigns tailored to high-engagement demographics

Promote high-performing content formats (Reels, Stories) to under-engaged users

Target users at risk of churn with personalized content and engagement nudges

Schedule marketing campaigns during high-traffic time slots

Leverage predictive analytics to flag and re-engage potential churners proactively

**Outcome**

This project provided the Marketing team with data-backed insights and visual tools to guide strategic decisions. The findings aim to help Meta boost Instagram's user engagement, enhance retention, and drive targeted user acquisition efforts.
